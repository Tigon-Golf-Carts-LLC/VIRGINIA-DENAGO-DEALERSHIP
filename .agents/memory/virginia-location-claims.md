name: Virginia location claims
description: Accuracy rule for physical dealership and statewide Virginia service-area messaging.
---

Treat Gloucester Point as the verified physical Virginia showroom. Describe Virginia Beach and the other named Virginia cities as service areas unless the user provides verified storefront addresses.

**Why:** The site needs strong statewide local relevance without inventing physical dealership locations or misleading customers and search engines.

**How to apply:** In page copy, maps, feeds, and structured data, reserve showroom/store/dealership-address language for Gloucester Point. Use area served or service area language for other Virginia communities.
